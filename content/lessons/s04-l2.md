# Preserve unmatched records

## What you will be able to do

Show one person without a matching company and retain them.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **preserve unmatched records** within joins and aggregations.

## Build the mental model

A left join keeps every left-side row and supplies nulls for missing right-side fields. Filters applied afterward can remove those unmatched rows. Decide whether a condition belongs to matching or to final output selection.

## Worked example

Read the example before running it. Identify the input facts or columns, the relationship being expressed, and the expected result.

```python
result = people.join(companies, "company_id", "left").select("name", "company_name")
```

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Filtering a right-side field after a left join can make it behave like an inner join for those rows.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
