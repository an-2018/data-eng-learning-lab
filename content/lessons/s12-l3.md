## What you will be able to do

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **recover safely** within spark deployment and observability.

## Build the mental model

Recovery begins with an immutable input snapshot and a known-good output version. Design retries to be idempotent, preserve failed artifacts, and promote only a validated complete result.
