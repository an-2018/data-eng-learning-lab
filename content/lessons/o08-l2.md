# Cardinality and identity

## What you will be able to do

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **cardinality and identity** within owl restrictions and reasoning.

## Build the mental model

Qualified cardinality counts values belonging to a specified class. OWL does not assume different names identify different individuals. With a maximum of one employer, two employer names may be inferred equal unless their difference or other facts causes a contradiction.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Two distinct IRI strings are not sufficient to prove two distinct individuals in OWL.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
