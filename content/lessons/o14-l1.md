# Bulk loading and serving

## What you will be able to do

List the checks needed before making a newly loaded dataset available.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **bulk loading and serving** within triplestore operations.

## Build the mental model

Bulk loaders optimize ingestion under documented operating conditions. Some require exclusive dataset access. Choose a loader based on the storage engine, graph size, and availability requirements, and verify the resulting dataset before serving queries.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A successful loader exit does not establish semantic completeness or validate constraints.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
