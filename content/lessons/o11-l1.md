# Choose what to reuse

## What you will be able to do

Compare two candidate terms against your competency questions and record the tradeoff.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **choose what to reuse** within reuse and ontology lifecycle.

## Build the mental model

Evaluate a vocabulary by semantic fit, identifier stability, governance, documentation, and maintenance. Reusing a familiar term with the wrong meaning creates integration debt. Record why a local term is needed when no suitable existing term exists.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Popularity does not make a vocabulary term semantically appropriate.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.

## Predict, then investigate

1. Compare two candidate terms against your competency questions and record the tradeoff.
2. Identify what happens when a relevant value or relationship is missing.
3. Change one fact or row and predict which part of the result changes.
4. Explain the result using the vocabulary introduced above.
