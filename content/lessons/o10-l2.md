# Logical combinations

## What you will be able to do

Test neither contact, email only, phone only, and both.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **logical combinations** within advanced shacl.

## Build the mental model

sh:or requires at least one alternative; sh:and requires every alternative; sh:xone requires exactly one. Build a truth table before coding a conditional business rule. A record satisfying two alternatives fails xone but passes or.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Using xone for email-or-phone rejects people who provide both.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
