# Retry unknown outcomes safely

## What you will be able to do

Describe an idempotency key and unknown-outcome procedure.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **retry unknown outcomes safely** within reliable ingestion, storage, and recovery.

## Build the mental model

A timeout can happen after commit. Use stable identifiers, operation records, and bounded retry to discover the outcome rather than assuming the transaction failed.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A generated identifier per retry can create duplicate business entities.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
