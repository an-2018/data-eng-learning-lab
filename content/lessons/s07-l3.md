## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **schema evolution deliberately** within files, formats, and partitioning.

## Build the mental model

Additive, nullable columns are usually easier to evolve than renamed or repurposed columns. Read and write behavior must be tested against old and new snapshots.
