# IRIs, literals, and datatypes

## What you will be able to do

Explain how "04"^^xsd:integer differs from the plain string "04".

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **iris, literals, and datatypes** within rdf foundations.

## Build the mental model

IRIs identify resources; literals carry values. An integer, a date, and a language-tagged label are different RDF terms. A lexical form that looks numeric is not enough to select a numeric datatype. Preserve source precision and choose an explicit timezone policy for timestamps.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Do not mix a language tag and a datatype annotation on the same literal.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
