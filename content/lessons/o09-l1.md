# Targets and focus nodes

## What you will be able to do

Add a Person instance and demonstrate that your target actually selects it.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **targets and focus nodes** within shacl foundations.

## Build the mental model

A NodeShape defines constraints for selected focus nodes. sh:targetClass selects class instances under SHACL target semantics, including the relevant subclass paths. A shape that targets no data may report conformance without testing the entities you intended.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A conforming report can mean that no focus nodes were selected.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
