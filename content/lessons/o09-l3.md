# Class and datatype constraints

## What you will be able to do

Show one invalid literal employer and one valid Company resource.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **class and datatype constraints** within shacl foundations.

## Build the mental model

Use sh:class when values should be resources of a class and sh:datatype when they should be typed literals. These are different contracts. Decide whether validation sees explicit facts or a separately materialized inference graph.

## Worked example

## A mistake worth recognizing

> An IRI whose text resembles a company name does not establish its class.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
