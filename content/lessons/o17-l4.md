# Bound paths and optionals

## What you will be able to do

Define a safe reporting-chain query and its operational limit.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **bound paths and optionals** within query optimization and benchmarking.

## Build the mental model

Unbounded paths and broad OPTIONAL branches can expand on cyclic or high-degree graphs. Bind endpoints, scope graphs, inspect plans, and preserve semantics while testing alternatives.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A property path is not automatically an indexed recursive lookup.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
