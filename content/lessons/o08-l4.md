# Profiles and reasoning contracts

## What you will be able to do

Define separate checks for parsing, profile validity, consistency, and intended entailments.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **profiles and reasoning contracts** within owl restrictions and reasoning.

## Build the mental model

OWL profiles restrict language features to support different computational tradeoffs. Record the supported profile and the reasoner version with an exercise. A rule-based subset cannot silently stand in for arbitrary OWL 2 DL reasoning.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A successful Turtle parse does not prove that an ontology satisfies OWL 2 DL global restrictions.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
