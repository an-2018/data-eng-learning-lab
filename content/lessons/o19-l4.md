# Isolate concurrent workloads

## What you will be able to do

Choose metrics and limits for load and interactive-query queues.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **isolate concurrent workloads** within reliable ingestion, storage, and recovery.

## Build the mental model

Document transaction behavior, back-pressure, worker limits, and workload separation. Keep bulk loads and expensive analytics from starving time-sensitive publication operations.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Unbounded retries under overload can amplify queue pressure.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
