# Paths and reachability

## What you will be able to do

Compare the outputs of reportsTo+ and reportsTo* on a three-person chain.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **paths and reachability** within advanced sparql.

## Build the mental model

Property paths traverse relationships. A plus means one or more steps; a star also includes zero steps. Sequence joins steps, alternatives choose predicates, and inverse paths traverse in the opposite direction. Think about cycles and endpoint bindings before using broad paths.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A zero-length path can return the starting node, even without an outgoing edge.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
