# Read an execution plan

## What you will be able to do

Inspect a group-by plan and identify where data must be rearranged.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **read an execution plan** within jobs, stages, partitions, and shuffles.

## Build the mental model

A logical plan describes intent; Spark turns it into a physical plan of jobs, stages, and tasks. Wide transformations such as many joins and aggregations often require a shuffle boundary.
