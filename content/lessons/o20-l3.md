# Respond to incidents

## What you will be able to do

Draft an incident timeline for a bad mapping release.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **respond to incidents** within production governance, security, and incident response.

## Build the mental model

Preserve bounded evidence, stop unsafe promotion, identify affected graph versions, revoke access when needed, restore a known-good release, and rehearse this procedure.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Deleting logs or graph data immediately can destroy the evidence needed for recovery.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
