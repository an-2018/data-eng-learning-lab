# Protect sensitive graph data

## What you will be able to do

Write a handling contract for employee contact data.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **protect sensitive graph data** within production governance, security, and incident response.

## Build the mental model

Classify sensitive predicates, minimize retention, encrypt backups and transport, and preserve access evidence without writing sensitive values to logs.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> An apparently anonymous graph can become identifiable when joined with other facts.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
