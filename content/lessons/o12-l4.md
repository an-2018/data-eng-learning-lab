# Identity assertions and provenance

## What you will be able to do

Design a reversible merge record and explain how an incorrect match would be repaired.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **identity assertions and provenance** within semantic integration and entity resolution.

## Build the mental model

Preserve source records even when a canonical entity is created. Record the mapping activity, evidence, rule version, and reviewer decision. Strong identity assertions can propagate many other facts through reasoning, so make them governed outputs.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Replacing source records with sameAs edges can discard the evidence needed to undo a bad match.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.

3. Change one fact or row and predict which part of the result changes.
