## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **partitions and parallel work** within spark execution model.

## Build the mental model

Partitions are units of data processing, and tasks operate on them. Increasing partition count can increase parallelism but also scheduling overhead. A tiny exercise dataset cannot prove how a large distributed workload will scale.
