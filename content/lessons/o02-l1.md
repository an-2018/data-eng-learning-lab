# Classes and instances

## What you will be able to do

Predict the type inferred for Ava and identify the two statements supporting it.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **classes and instances** within rdfs and vocabulary design.

## Build the mental model

A class groups resources. Typing Ava as an Engineer states membership; subclassing Engineer under Person states that every Engineer is a Person. The class resource and its members play different roles. Choose names that make this distinction clear.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A subclass statement is not an assertion that the class itself is a person.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
