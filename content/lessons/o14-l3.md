# Retries and ambiguous outcomes

## What you will be able to do

Describe how to recover when the response is lost after commit.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **retries and ambiguous outcomes** within triplestore operations.

## Build the mental model

A timeout can occur after a server commits but before the client receives confirmation. An idempotency key and a persisted operation record let the client discover the outcome. Retry transient failures with bounds and backoff; surface invalid input immediately.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Retrying every exception can duplicate non-idempotent operations and hide deterministic failures.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
