# Control joins and cardinality

## What you will be able to do

Repair a disconnected join and explain its output grain.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **control joins and cardinality** within query optimization and benchmarking.

## Build the mental model

Shared variables connect patterns. Missing joins make Cartesian products; DISTINCT can hide output duplication while retaining expensive work. Estimate intermediate cardinalities and aggregate at the intended grain.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A label join after grouping can change the count when an entity has multiple labels.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
