# Migration and provenance

## What you will be able to do

Describe the evidence needed to reverse an erroneous entity merge.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **migration and provenance** within governance and release.

## Build the mental model

A migration transforms data under a named mapping version. Retain provenance and a rollback strategy. If a transformation loses information, rollback may require the source snapshot rather than a simple inverse query.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Not every transformation can be inverted from its output alone.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.

## Predict, then investigate

1. Describe the evidence needed to reverse an erroneous entity merge.
2. Identify what happens when a relevant value or relationship is missing.
3. Change one fact or row and predict which part of the result changes.
4. Explain the result using the vocabulary introduced above.
