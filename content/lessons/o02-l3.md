# Property hierarchies

## What you will be able to do

Write a query that benefits from general contributions when inference is enabled.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **property hierarchies** within rdfs and vocabulary design.

## Build the mental model

A subproperty lets a specialized relationship imply a general one. Every managesProject link may count as contributesTo, while a contribution does not necessarily imply management. Keep the implication directional.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Subproperty relationships do not automatically define inverse relationships.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
