# Identity and lifecycle

## What you will be able to do

Decide which identifiers survive a product rename or an order correction.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **identity and lifecycle** within modeling method.

## Build the mental model

Make something a first-class resource when it must be identified, linked, reused, tracked through changes, or given its own evidence. Not every noun needs a class. A scalar measurement usually remains a literal unless it needs qualifiers or independent identity.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A mutable label is usually a poor basis for a persistent entity identifier.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.

## Predict, then investigate

1. Decide which identifiers survive a product rename or an order correction.
2. Identify what happens when a relevant value or relationship is missing.
3. Change one fact or row and predict which part of the result changes.
4. Explain the result using the vocabulary introduced above.
