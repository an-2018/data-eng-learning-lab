# Taxonomy versus entity model

## What you will be able to do

Explain whether Brand, Category, and OrderLine need independent identity in your application.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **taxonomy versus entity model** within modeling method.

## Build the mental model

A category can be a reusable concept in a controlled vocabulary. A Brand may need its own identity because products link to it and its owner can change. SKOS broader relationships and OWL subclass statements express different meanings.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A broader category does not automatically mean every instance is an OWL subclass instance.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
