## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **inspect the plan** within spark execution model.

## Build the mental model

A logical plan describes requested transformations; the physical plan describes selected execution operations. Inspect scans, projections, exchanges, and joins. Use the plan to form hypotheses, then measure on a suitable dataset.
