# Mappings have different strength

## What you will be able to do

Explain what evidence would be required before strengthening a mapping.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **mappings have different strength** within reuse and ontology lifecycle.

## Build the mental model

An OWL equivalence statement is a logical commitment, not a fuzzy match score. SKOS mapping predicates carry their own semantics. Preserve weaker mappings where source concepts differ, and keep evidence alongside the mapping decision.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A close textual match does not justify equivalentClass or sameAs.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
