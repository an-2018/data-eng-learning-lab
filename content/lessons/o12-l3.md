# Confidence and review

## What you will be able to do

Explain the consequences of raising an auto-accept threshold on precision and recall.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **confidence and review** within semantic integration and entity resolution.

## Build the mental model

Separate candidate generation, scoring, and decision thresholds. A score is meaningful only with calibration and a defined population. Use accept, reject, and review bands, and evaluate false merges as well as missed matches.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A decimal score is not automatically a probability that the identity is correct.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
