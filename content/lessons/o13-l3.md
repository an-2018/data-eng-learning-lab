# Idempotent transformations

## What you will be able to do

Run the update twice and compare the resulting graph, not just the success response.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **idempotent transformations** within ingestion and publication.

## Build the mental model

A retry should not create a second representation of the same business fact. Stable IRIs and replacement of a well-defined snapshot graph can make retries predictable. Blank nodes generated afresh on each attempt need special care.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Appending fresh anonymous association nodes on every retry breaks idempotency.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
