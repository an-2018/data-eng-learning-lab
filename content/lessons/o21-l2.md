# Represent affinity evidence and windows

## What you will be able to do

Record a versioned affinity calculation for one device and genre.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **represent affinity evidence and windows** within media audience knowledge graphs and shacl.

## Build the mental model

An affinity needs identity when it has a score, time window, method version, evidence, and provenance. This allows recalculation and comparison without overwriting history.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A direct likesGenre edge loses score, measurement window, and derivation method.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
