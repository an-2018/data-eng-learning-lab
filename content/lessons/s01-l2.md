## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **lazy transformations and actions** within spark execution model.

## Build the mental model

Transformations build a plan. An action asks Spark to produce a result and triggers execution. A chain of selects and filters does not necessarily create separate full scans because the optimizer can combine work.

## Worked example

Read the example before running it. Identify the input facts or columns, the relationship being expressed, and the expected result.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> A transformation line is not necessarily a Spark job boundary.
