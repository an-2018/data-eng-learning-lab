## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **control partition counts** within jobs, stages, partitions, and shuffles.

## Build the mental model

Partition count is a resource decision. Too few partitions underuse cores; too many make scheduler overhead dominate. Measure records, bytes, skew, and task duration before tuning.
