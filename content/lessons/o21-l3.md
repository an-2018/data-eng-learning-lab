# Preserve media and identity distinctions

## What you will be able to do

Create an evidence-bearing device-to-household link without asserting identity.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **preserve media and identity distinctions** within media audience knowledge graphs and shacl.

## Build the mental model

Content sponsorship, ad exposure, brand web interaction, and purchase are separate signals. A device is not automatically a person, and shared IP evidence does not prove a household.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Collapsing identity evidence into owl:sameAs discards uncertainty and temporal validity.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
