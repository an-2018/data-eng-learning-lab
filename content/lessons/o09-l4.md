# Read validation reports

## What you will be able to do

For a missing employeeId, identify the focus node, path, and cardinality rule.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **read validation reports** within shacl foundations.

## Build the mental model

A validation result identifies a focus node, a constraint component, and often a path and offending value. Use these fields to explain failures without relying on engine-specific English messages. Distinguish a malformed shapes graph from invalid data.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Changing a validation message does not repair a failing constraint.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.
