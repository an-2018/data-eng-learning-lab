# Publish atomically

## What you will be able to do

Place promotion in one transaction and predict what a concurrent reader observes.

## The problem

You are working with workforce and project data that will be used by another team. Your result must have a clear meaning, survive incomplete records, and be reproducible. This lesson focuses on **publish atomically** within ingestion and publication.

## Build the mental model

Within one transactional dataset, readers should see either the old published graph or the complete new graph. Keep validation and promotion boundaries explicit. Atomicity across unrelated services requires a broader design and is not supplied by SPARQL syntax alone.

## A mistake worth recognizing

> Two independently committed requests can expose an intermediate empty graph.

Construct a small counterexample that would expose this mistake. Prefer three carefully chosen records to a large dataset whose behavior is hard to explain.

## Predict, then investigate

1. Place promotion in one transaction and predict what a concurrent reader observes.
2. Identify what happens when a relevant value or relationship is missing.
3. Change one fact or row and predict which part of the result changes.
4. Explain the result using the vocabulary introduced above.
